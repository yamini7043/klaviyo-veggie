Happy Response

When a customer submits positive feedback:

The customer enters the Happy branch of the conditional split.
A profile property is updated:
Happy Customers = true
Customers with this property are added to the Happy Customers segment.
This segment can later be used for:
Review request campaigns
Loyalty campaigns
Referral programs
VIP customer nurturing

Unhappy Response

When a customer submits negative feedback:

The customer enters the Unhappy branch of the conditional split.
A profile property is updated:
Needs Support = true
Customers with this property are added to the Needs Support segment.
The unhappy feedback option will directs customers to the customer service/replacement support page(will do this in future automatically redirect to customer support when the form is submitted).
A future Customer Service Follow-Up Flow can be triggered when someone enters the Needs Support segment.
This allows the support team to proactively contact customers and resolve issues.
