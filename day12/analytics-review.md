Summary

What Is Working

Abandoned cart reminders are generating engagement.

The “You left something in your cart?” email is the only flow email with measurable clicks (5.26% CTR).

No spam complaint problem but the email either lands in promotions or updates.

Complaint rates are currently 0%, which is excellent.

Unsubscribe rates are low.

This suggests recipients are not finding the messaging excessively annoying yet.

What Needs Improvement

Bounce rates are far too high.

Multiple emails and campaigns exceed the recommended 2% threshold. This is the most urgent issue.

Post-purchase engagement is weak.

Most post-order emails have 0% click rate and very low open rates.

Sample sizes are extremely small.

Many flows have fewer than 20 deliveries, making the metrics unstable.

Generic subject lines are underperforming.

Emails such as “Getting started” provide little incentive to open.

3 Specific Actions for Next Month

Clean the list and verify sending setup.

Remove hard bounces, suppress invalid addresses, and verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC configuration.

Rewrite post-purchase subjects to be specific.

Examples: “Your order is confirmed, {{ first_name }}” and “Your fresh produce is on the way”.

Continue A/B testing with a single variable.

For the restock email, test “Reorder Now” vs “Restock My Kitchen” and evaluate on click rate after each version reaches at least 100 sends.
