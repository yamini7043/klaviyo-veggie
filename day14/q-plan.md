# Additional Flows & Opportunities for Next Quarter

## 1. New Product Alert Flow

### Trigger

Product Added to Catalog

### Audience

Customers interested in that category

### Example

New Mango Variety Added

New Organic Vegetables Available

New Flower Collection Added

### Goal

Drive early sales on newly added products.

---

## 2. New Offer / Discount Flow

### Trigger

Special Offer Created

### Audience

Engaged Subscribers

### Example

10% Off Summer Fruits

Weekend Vegetable Sale

Festival Special Discounts

### Goal

Generate quick sales during promotional periods.

---

## 3. Back In Stock Flow

### Trigger

Inventory Returns Above Threshold

### Audience

Customers who:

- Viewed product
- Added product to cart
- Purchased previously

### Goal

Recover missed sales opportunities.

---

## 4. Price Drop Alert Flow

### Trigger

Product Price Reduced

### Audience

Interested Customers

### Example

Alphonso Mangoes Now ₹50 Off

### Goal

Increase conversion from interested shoppers.

---

## 5. VIP Early Access Flow

### Trigger

New Product Launch or Festival Sale

### Audience

VIP Customers

### Goal

Reward loyal customers with early access.

---
