Farm2Home SMS Policy

SMS Frequency

Maximum 2–3 SMS messages per customer per month.
Delivery updates may be sent as needed for active orders.

Approved SMS Uses

Order confirmations
Delivery updates
Flash sales (maximum 1 promotional SMS per month)
Restock alerts
Important service notifications

Customer Consent

SMS messages will only be sent to customers who have explicitly opted in to receive SMS communications.

Opt-Out

Every promotional SMS must include:
Reply STOP to opt out.

Quiet Hours

No SMS messages will be sent:
Before 9:00 AM
After 8:00 PM

Content Guidelines

Keep messages clear, relevant, and concise.
Focus on timely information and high-value offers.
Avoid excessive promotional messaging.
